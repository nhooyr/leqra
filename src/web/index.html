Drive and aim

Mouse (Player 1)
Move the pointer over the maze to aim. Left-click to fire or hold to keep firing. Use the keyboard to drive, and press a turn key to switch back to keyboard aiming.

Drag the joystick to steer and drive. Tap or hold Fire to shoot.

Bank shots around corners and use walls for cover. Your own shots and grenade blasts leave your tank unharmed.

Drive over a glowing pickup to collect it. Laser previews its ricochet path, grenades can detonate when you press Fire again, and a rare Starman lets you pass through walls unharmed for a few seconds.

  • Elimination · Be the last tank or team standing.
  • Capture the Flag · Bring the enemy flag home while yours is at base.
  • King of the Hill · Control the hill to earn points.
  • Survival · Fight off waves of enemy tanks with your squad.

Select a mode under Room settings, then press Play to start.
