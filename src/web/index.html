Drive over a glowing pickup to collect it.

  • Laser · Fires an instant ricocheting beam with a dotted aim guide.
  • Grenade · Homes in and explodes on impact, another fire press, or after 10 seconds.
  • Starman · For 4–8 seconds, you’re invincible, can drive through walls, destroy tanks you ram, and fire with unlimited ammo and no cooldown.

There are more power-ups. Check the sidebar for the full list and details.

  • Elimination · Be the last tank or team standing.
  • Capture the Flag · Bring the enemy flag home while yours is at base.
  • King of the Hill · Control the hill to earn points.
  • Survival · Fight off waves of enemy tanks with your squad.
  • Infinity · Roam an unbounded maze where tanks respawn. The Infinity switch above Play joins the public arena. Choose Infinity in Room settings to play with your room roster and set whether roaming bots can spawn.

To play a room match, turn off Infinity, choose a mode in Room settings, and press Play.
