# Power-ups

Drive over a glowing pickup to collect it. Weapons use the same Fire control. A new weapon replaces the equipped weapon. Shield, Super Speed, Ghost, and Starman can coexist with weapons and each other.

Weapons and most buffs last **10 seconds** on Compact, Standard, and Large, or **15 seconds** on Huge, Giant, and Ultra Wide. Projectile range, flight time, and grenade fuses have separate limits.

| Pickup | Effect | Charges or duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cannon | Fires large, fast rounds through internal walls. They bounce off the arena rim. | 3 shots or 10/15 seconds |
| Ghost | Lets you drive through internal walls. The rim stays solid. | 10/15 seconds |
| Laser | Fires an instant ricocheting beam with a dotted guide that previews its path. Range is one maze perimeter. | 3 shots or 10/15 seconds |
| Homing missile | Tracks visible opponents and bounces off walls. Range is half the maze perimeter. | 3 missiles or 10/15 seconds |
| Grenade | Homes in on visible opponents like a missile and slows near the end of its 10-second fuse. Explodes on contact with another tank, when you press Fire again, or when the fuse expires. Walls block the 220-unit blast. | 3 throws or 10/15 seconds |
| Super Speed | Adds 65% movement speed and 25% turn speed per stack. | Up to 5 stacks with a shared 10/15-second timer |
| Machine gun | Fires small, fast ricocheting rounds while Fire is held. Range is a quarter of the maze perimeter. | 3 seconds of firing within 10/15 seconds |
| Shotgun | Fires three fast pellets in a spread. | 5 volleys or 10/15 seconds |
| Shield | Adds a ring that absorbs a damaging hit. | Up to 5 charges with a shared 10/15-second timer |
| Starman | A gold star and rainbow glow mark temporary invulnerability and wall phasing. | 3 seconds |

## Combat rules

Your own shots and grenade blasts leave your tank and shields unharmed. Friendly fire is a room rule. The next Fire press detonates an armed grenade even if you changed weapons.

Ghost and Starman let you cross internal walls. If phasing ends inside a wall, the game moves you to a safe position. Ordinary weapons need a clear muzzle. Cannon can shoot through internal walls.

Starman protects existing shields and refreshes on another pickup. It pauses objective interaction while invulnerability is active. With all pickups enabled, about **1 in 27** spawns is Starman. Shield and Super Speed each have six times its spawn weight, and other pickups each have twice its weight.

Use **Room settings → Rules** to change pickup frequency or which types can spawn.
