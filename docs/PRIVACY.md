# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy applies to leqra on the web, iOS, and Android.

## Information we store

leqra does not ask for your real name, email address, or other contact details.
Your chosen callsign, tank color, and score are visible to other players,
including on the public Infinity leaderboard. Avoid using personal information
in your callsign.

We store your callsign and public Infinity score, along with the limited
information needed to maintain them: your tank color, internal game identifiers,
a hash of your randomly generated account key, and your last activity time.
These records let you keep your callsign and recover your score across sessions.

Your device also saves account keys, callsigns, game settings, and local gameplay
records. Online play temporarily processes connection information, including
your IP address, to connect players and prevent abuse. Game state and any
in-room chat are shared with the relevant participants during play.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, or sell your data.

## Deleting your data

Use **Release callsign** in **Account** to delete an account's callsign and score.
Accounts also expire after one calendar month without signing in or playing
online. Hashes of invalidated account keys remain to prevent those keys from
being reused.

Device storage is separate from server records. Clearing site data or removing
the app does not itself delete your server account.

## Questions

For questions about this policy, use the
[leqra issue tracker](https://github.com/nhooyr/leqra/issues).
Do not post account keys or other private information there.
